Check a Workroom release record

Paste the signed record you were sent, and choose the document and the source workbooks it names. Everything is checked in this page, on your computer: nothing is uploaded and no server is contacted.

The record shows the key it was signed with. Whether you trust that key is your decision: ask the sender to read you its fingerprint.

What a pass proves: the record was made by the holder of that key, and the files are byte for byte what it names. What it does not prove: that the figures are true, that the sources are right, or who holds the key.
